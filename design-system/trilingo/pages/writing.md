# Writing — Phase 6

Product register; Operate for draft saving and Read for Japanese composition. Desktop uses a broad editor with a quieter task/reference column; mobile places the prompt, editor and saving actions in one natural column. The signature is a clear Japanese writing surface with an optional square formation grid. Use MASTER's teal/plum/ink/paper tokens and local Segoe UI/Yu Gothic/Meiryo; no downloads or independent palette.

Practice > Writing opens input help and freely selected beginner tasks. Formation has its own route/tab, keeping pointer controls out of the composition editor. Constrained typed checks use the existing exercise renderer and backend grader. Open writing shows not assessed or self-reviewed, never an exact-answer score. Three Stage B tasks and their model/revision references remain draft until qualified review.

An acknowledged revision is Saved; current edits are Unsaved. Autosave pauses during IME composition. A conflict keeps incoming and saved texts, offers a comparison and lets the learner deliberately save a merged/new revision. Retries reuse the original operation ID. Autosave earns no XP; committed new practice submissions earn normal session XP, without quotas.

Formation uses normalized SVG coordinates, an original draft template, numbered start/direction instructions and a keyboard stroke-selection alternative. Mouse/touch/stylus share pointer events. Undo, confirmed clear, replay and optional sample saving/deletion are explicit. Geometric tracing feedback and freehand self-report never claim handwriting recognition. Raw stroke storage is opt-in; no raw drawing goes into browser recovery storage.

The Windows input guide remains a draft until checked against current official documentation; physical IME, touch and stylus tests remain open. Rendered responsive/error/recovery/keyboard audits are pending actual implementation.
